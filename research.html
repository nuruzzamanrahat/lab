Research

Our active projects, methods, and the real-world problems we are trying to solve.

Theme 1

NLP & Low-Resource Languages

Building tools for the 300+ million Bangla speakers — and methods that transfer to other under-resourced languages.

Active

Bangla Semantic Parsing

Building a BNLP benchmark and fine-tuning multilingual models for structured meaning representation in Bangla.

Lead: Dr. Samira Islam · 2023 – present
Active

Low-Resource Machine Translation

Adapting large LLMs to Bangla–English and Bangla–Hindi translation with minimal labelled data using back-translation and retrieval augmentation.

Lead: Tasneem Akter · 2024 – present

Theme 2

Medical & Bioinformatics AI

Applying machine learning to healthcare problems where data is scarce and stakes are high.

Active

Federated Medical Imaging

Training chest X-ray classifiers across hospitals without sharing raw patient data, using differential privacy and federated aggregation.

Lead: Rafiq Karim · 2023 – present
Active

Drug Interaction Prediction

Graph neural networks over molecular graphs to predict adverse drug-drug interactions, with interpretability via attention rollout.

Lead: Nadia Begum · 2022 – present

Theme 3

Remote Sensing & Climate

Using satellite data and ML to monitor environmental change across South Asia.

Active

Real-Time Flood Mapping

SAR + optical fusion for near real-time flood extent mapping in Bangladesh, in collaboration with BWDB and SPARRSO.

Lead: Mehedi Hossain · ICT Division funded · 2023 – 2025
Completed

Land Use Change Detection (2017–2022)

Multi-temporal Landsat analysis to quantify urban sprawl and cropland loss in the Dhaka metro region.
