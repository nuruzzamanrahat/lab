Research Areas

Our work spans three interconnected themes, all aimed at meaningful real-world impact.

NLP & Low-Resource Languages

Building language models for Bangla and other under-resourced languages, making NLP tools accessible beyond English-centric research.

Learn more →

Medical & Bioinformatics AI

Applying deep learning to medical imaging, drug interaction prediction, and genomic data for healthcare in resource-constrained environments.

Learn more →

Remote Sensing & Climate

Using satellite imagery and ML to monitor floods, land use, and environmental change — with a particular focus on South Asian geography.
